Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Homelab PA is a private application used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Calendar solely to create and read calendar events at the owner's request.

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own self-hosted server.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising or analytics.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Homelab PA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
